If you don't want to take the ferry to the Isle Of Wight then you can try the Hovercraft, however please note this is only for foot passengers.

Unfortunately, there are no options to the Isle Of Wight that don't involve going by water - unless you have a Helicopter

Why You Can Trust Our Ferries to the Isle of Wight Information

This tool was built from over a decade of first-hand travel and we have taken ferries and the hoover craft to the Isle of Wight a number of times in the past few years. Enjoying long weekends and day drips to the island.

To build this tool, we combined our real-world experience with structured, up-to-date data on crossing times, availability, costs, route options, and port accessibility.

We hope this tool helps you find the right route for you. Have a wonderful trip! — [Carl - aka Travelbetter Dad]
