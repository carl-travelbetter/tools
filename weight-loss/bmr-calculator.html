🧮 BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) Calculator

Use our BMR calculator to easily understand how many calories your body burns every day while at rest - simply put, when you are not moving.

This number is useful in helping you meet your weight and fitness goals.

This is required as there are two different calcualtions for BMR depending on sex.

About Travelbetter

Travelbetter is a family travel and life style project created to support quiet, anxious and neurodiverse family travellers. We believe travel should feel calm, kind and accessible — not overwhelming. Through simple tools, thoughtful guides and lived experience, we help families plan trips that truly work for them.

Our mission is to help families Explore More and Worry Less
