How to Convert Miles into Kilometres

Here are some of the ways to convert fro Miles to Kilometres.

Multiply by 1.609 - Multiple the miles M by 1.609 to get the kilometre KM, KM = M*1.609

Use 5/8 - For every five miles it is approximately 8 kilometres. Therefore, take your miles M and divide them by 5, then multiply the result by 8 to get the kilometer KM value.
