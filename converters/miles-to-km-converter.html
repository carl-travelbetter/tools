How To Convert Miles Into Kilometres

Here are the formulas to convert from Miles to Kilometres.

Multiply Miles By 1.609 - Multiple the miles M by 1.609 to get the kilometre KM, KM = M*1.609

Use 5/8 ratio - For every five miles it is approximately 8 kilometres. Therefore, take your miles M and divide them by 5, then multiply the result by 8 to get the kilometer KM value.
