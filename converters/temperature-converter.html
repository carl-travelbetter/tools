Temperature Conversion Formulas

To Convert From Celsius to Fahrenheit

Fahrenheit = ((Celsius / 5)*9)+32

Worked Example

Celisus = 20°C

Fahrenheit = ((20° / 5)*9) + 32

Fahrenheit = (4*9) + 32

Fahrenheit = 36 + 32

Fahrenheit = 68°F

To Convert From Celsius to Kelvin

Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15

Worked Example

Celisus = 20°C

Kelvin = 20° + 273.15

Kelvin = 293.15k

To Convert From Fahrenheit to Celsius

Celsius = ((Fahrenheit-32)*5)/9

Worked Example

Fahrenheit = 50°

Celsius = ((50-32)*5)/9

Celsius = ((18)*5)/9

Celsius = 90/9

Celsius = 10°C

To Convert From Fahrenheit to Kelvin

The easiest way to do this is in two stages, first convert from Farhenheit to Celsius, then from Celsius to Kelvin

Stage 1 -> Celsius = ((Fahrenheit-32)*5)/9;

Stage 2 -> Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15

Worked Example

Fahrenheit = 50°
